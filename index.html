About

Reliable Support. Organized Work. Better Business.

Hi, I’m Roque!

As a Virtual Assistant, my goal is to streamline your day-to-day operations by handling time-consuming administrative tasks, allowing you to focus on what matters most—growing your business and achieving your goals.

I specialize in General Virtual Assistance, including email and calendar management, scheduling, data entry, online research, administrative support, and project coordination. I’m reliable, highly organized, detail-oriented, and proactive, with a strong commitment to delivering accurate, efficient, and high-quality work.

I help business owners stay organized and focused on growth through reliable General Virtual Assistance, Social Media Management, and Data Entry. With a Bachelor’s degree in Marketing and HubSpot Marketing Software and CRM / Sales Software certifications, I bring strong organization, communication, attention to detail, and creativity. Today I work as a Freelance Virtual Assistant for USA clients, and my experience also includes roles as a Real Estate Agent at eXp Realty, Logistics Coordinator at PODS, and Reservations &amp; Sales Specialist at Choice Hotels, plus sales roles at AT&amp;T and IHG. I streamline daily operations so you can focus on growing your business.

My approach is simple: take tasks off your plate, keep your operations organized, and help you work more efficiently.
